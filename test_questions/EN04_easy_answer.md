**EN04 – könnyű**

**Kérdés**
What types of text should a benchmark include to test false and synthetic content detection across low-resource languages?

**Válasz**
Based on the provided articles, a comprehensive benchmark for detecting false and synthetic content across low-resource languages (such as BLUFF) should include four types of text:

1. Human-written fact-checked content [\[2603.00634\]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00634).
2. LLM-generated content [\[2603.00634\]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00634).
3. LLM-translated text [\[2603.00634\]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00634).
4. Hybrid human-LLM text [\[2603.00634\]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00634).
